<h1 class="p-name">Everyday apps</h1>

The apps and services I use regularly.

## Browsing and communication

- Browser: [Firefox](https://www.firefox.com/).
- Search: [Kagi](https://kagi.com/).
- Email, calendar and contacts: [Fastmail](https://www.fastmail.com/).
- Messaging: [Telegram](https://t.me/darccio), [WhatsApp](https://wa.me/+34665269218) and [Signal](https://signal.me/#eu/F81LnR48CdEJHZDlsV4ixYV0s4E0Dg6Jsp4RptJGk1bsna6g1HdDio5IfT8oAxkx).

## Organisation

- Notes: [Apple Notes](https://apps.apple.com/app/notes/id1110145109).
- Tasks: [Reminders](https://apps.apple.com/app/reminders/id1108187841).

## Development

- Editor: [Visual Studio Code](https://code.visualstudio.com/).
- Terminal: [Ghostty](https://ghostty.org/).
- Shell: [Bash](https://www.gnu.org/software/bash/) or [Zsh](https://www.zsh.org/).

## Data and security

- Passwords: [1Password](https://1password.com/).
- Storage: my own [Synology](https://www.synology.com/) NAS, with the setup explained in [Monitoring a Synology NAS with Datadog](/en/posts/monitoring-synology-nas-with-datadog/).
- Backups: [BorgBase](https://www.borgbase.com/).

## Reading and entertainment

- RSS: [NewsBlur](https://newsblur.com/).
- Music, podcasts and video: [Spotify](https://www.spotify.com/) and [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/), for now.
