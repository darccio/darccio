<h1 class="p-name">Languages</h1>

Catalan is the default language of this website and its personal pages. There is also an [English section](/en/), with [articles in English](/en/posts/). I also publish articles in Spanish. The documentation for my [open-source projects](/en/projects/) is mainly in English.

You can find articles in:

- [The archive in all languages](/posts/), with a language label on each entry.
- [The Catalan archive](/posts/ca/).
- [Articles in English](/en/posts/).
- [Articles in Spanish](/es/posts/).

Some articles have versions in more than one language. Each translation keeps its own URL. The `ca`, `en` and `es` badges in the archives show which language each article uses.

The [subscriptions](/en/feeds/) include a feed with all articles and separate feeds in English and Spanish.
