## Who am I?

<p class="p-note note">Born in <time datetime="1985-09-23" class="dt-bday">1985</time>. A <span class="p-role role">Computer Systems Engineer</span> from the <a class="p-org org h-card" href="https://www.uoc.edu/">UOC</a> and a <span class="p-job-title p-role role">Senior Software Engineer</span> at <a class="p-org org h-card" href="https://datadoghq.com">Datadog</a>. A <span class="p-role role">former councillor</span> with <span class="p-org org">Decidim Igualada</span> and a <span class="p-role role">developer</span> of <a class="u-url url" href="https://github.com/darccio" rel="me">free software</a>. I have been an <span class="p-role role">organiser</span> of the <a class="p-org org h-card" href="https://www.meetup.com/es-ES/IGD-Tech-Drinks/">IGD Tech &amp; Drinks</a> meetup.</p>

I write about technology and its impact on society, [internal democracy](/en/posts/introduction-liquid-organizations/) and politics in general. You can read [the article archive](/en/posts/) and my newsletter, [Rizoma](https://rizoma.dario.cat/).

I also share <a class="u-url url" href="https://speakerdeck.com/dario" rel="me">talks and workshops</a>. [Projects](/en/projects/) brings together my free software work; [Now](/en/now/) covers what I am working on.

My [CV](/cv/) covers my professional experience, education and projects. It is also available in [MAC JSON format](/CV/MAC.json).
