<h1 class="p-name">Feeds</h1>

RSS lets you follow publications in a feed reader. Add a feed's address to receive articles without having to visit each site.

## Articles on this site

- [All articles: RSS](/feed.xml), in Catalan, Spanish and English.
- [Articles in English: RSS](/en/feed.xml).
- [Articles in Spanish: RSS](/es/feed.xml).

The feeds contain full article text and up to 50 entries. You can also read [the article archive](/en/posts/) on the site.

## Rizoma

[Rizoma](https://rizoma.dario.cat/) is my newsletter in Catalan. New editions are published on Substack and have [their own RSS feed](https://rizoma.dario.cat/feed).

The four editions from 2021 remain in [this site's full archive](/posts/) and are included in the combined RSS feed. New Substack editions have their own subscription and are not duplicated in this feed.
