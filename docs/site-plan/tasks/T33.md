# T33 Recover older articles from other sites

Recover Dario's older articles from their original publishers or archive.org, preserving their text, language, source evidence and associated media as reviewed local articles. Inventory and source review precede imports; this backlog addition starts no scraping or publication.

**Repository:** site; zas only for later generic features. **Phase:** Content recovery. **Depends on:** T04, T02, T31 (already available).

**Status:** Queued. **Subagent:** /root/t33_article_recovery (task planning).

## Repository findings and scope

- Start the inventory from existing article sources, external citations in `posts/index.md` and [publication provenance](../../publication-provenance.md); add owner-provided publisher/profile URLs as they become available. Existing source references establish leads, not a complete list of recoverable works.
- Record stable original URLs, author evidence, titles, languages, publisher and canonical/translation relationships. Reconcile against local articles before retrieval so later copies of existing work do not become duplicate posts. Translations remain distinct documents with their own language and URLs.
- Prefer an accessible original article; otherwise retrieve a suitable Wayback snapshot and its associated media. Record unavailable sources, conflicting versions and missing assets explicitly. Recovery and conversion use saved evidence; builds and runtime require no archive.org access.
- Preserve the author's wording, headings, links, code, captions and language. Convert article content to Markdown, remove publisher navigation, ads, scripts and archive replay chrome, and store reviewed media locally with corrected local references. Record substantive source differences rather than silently rewriting or combining versions.
- Retain existing public site URLs, RSS GUIDs, article syndication settings and approved Standard.site identities. Review new local paths separately; keep original publisher and snapshot attribution visible and preserve source links in provenance.

## Acceptance

- Every inventory item has a disposition: already local, recovered and reviewed, pending evidence/rights, or unavailable. Each proposed import has an author/source review, Markdown text and necessary local media, or an explicit reviewed account of missing assets.
- A private provenance record identifies original and retrieved URLs, snapshot URLs/capture times where used, retrieval time, hashes of saved source/media bytes, publication evidence and precision, conversion changes, attribution, rights and missing assets. Preserve publisher terms, coauthor and third-party media rights; recovery does not relicense them.
- Apply T04's publication conventions: distinguish original publication from later copies, capture dates and retrieval dates; retain sourced instants and partial precision. Never fabricate a clock/timezone or infer publication from Git, archive headings or capture times. Owner-confirmed values and adopted assumptions remain labeled separately from recovered evidence.
- New recovered posts default to `feed: false` and `atproto: false` until dates/provenance are reviewed and syndication is explicitly selected. Eligibility never expands the T30 allowlist or publishes records implicitly. Existing articles retain their current exclusions, GUIDs and publishing state.
- The same reviewed canonical, language, summary, tags and date metadata supplies RSS, microformats, JSON-LD, Markdown companions and local Standard.site plans under their existing eligibility rules. Imports preserve source-derived archive completeness and all guarded-build protections.

## Verification

- Review each recovered body against saved original/snapshot evidence, including code, links, captions and author attribution; check local media hashes/references and document unresolved differences or missing assets. Reconcile inventory counts by work and by language/document, including already-local copies and unresolved items.
- After each proposed import batch, run the existing guarded build and T31 consistency gate; compare archive inventories, canonical paths, publication precision and existing RSS GUIDs/syndication state. Confirm excluded recovered posts stay absent from RSS and Standard.site selection, existing URLs remain present, and private evidence stays outside deploy output.
- If retrieval tooling is necessary, use a dry-run manifest/diff before writing and make unchanged reruns produce no source/asset changes. Later substantive fixtures cover article extraction, replay chrome, missing media, canonical/translation duplicates, partial/conflicting dates and failure without partial imports; no scraper or recovery tests are introduced by this planning item.
- With saved recovery evidence and cached build dependencies, build without origin/archive.org connectivity. Check local asset availability and preserve the previous deployment if validation fails.

## Owner and external requirements

- The owner has not supplied every external source URL. Repository evidence is enough to begin the later inventory; additional URLs refine its scope without blocking this backlog entry.
- Resolve disputed authorship, publication evidence, republishing rights and media rights before the affected public import. Known partial dates use the existing precision conventions with syndication excluded. Items with no verified calendar date remain in the unpublished inventory until they can be represented accurately. Approval of a local article is separate from selection for RSS or Standard.site publication.

## Commits and operational actions

Each Git commit includes the implementation, its necessary tests and its documentation. Zas commits become focused PRs against current upstream master. External project source changes require their own authorized repository work; preparation artifacts belong in the site repository.

- **site** · `docs(articles): inventory historical sources and recovery evidence`: Private inventory, saved-evidence/provenance conventions and per-source review outcomes; no automatic publication.
- **site**, only if needed · `feat(tools): recover reviewed historical article sources`: Small site-specific Go retrieval/conversion tooling with dry-run, idempotence, substantive fixtures and operator instructions. Generic generator features would be scoped into later upstream Zas PRs; no build-time fetching.
- **site** · `feat(posts): recover <reviewed article or source batch>`: Small article/source-review commits containing reviewed Markdown, local assets, provenance, metadata and archive changes together. Keep syndication exclusions until explicit selection; any later Standard.site proposal follows T30's reviewed allowlist and publishing procedure.

## Rollback

Revert an affected import commit and its archive/asset additions together, retaining other articles, established URLs, GUIDs and publishing state. Revert optional recovery tooling independently. Restore prior successful build output if necessary; remote record deletion, if later publication occurred, follows its separately approved explicit-target procedure and cannot revoke third-party copies.
