# T34 Recover Facebook, Twitter and Bluesky posts

Recover selected public posts and media authored by Dario from Facebook, Twitter and Bluesky, reconstructing evidenced Twitter and Bluesky threads and preserving related posts as clearly labeled collections when appropriate. This queued plan starts no retrieval, export parsing or publication.

**Repository:** site; zas only for later generic features. **Phase:** Content recovery. **Depends on:** T33, T04, T02, T31.

**Status:** Queued. **Subagent:** /root/t34_social_recovery (task planning).

## Repository findings and scope

- Reuse T33's inventory, saved-evidence, provenance, sanitization and reviewed Markdown/media import approach. Start with owner-selected accounts, public source URLs, time ranges and available exports; reconcile against existing local articles and later cross-platform copies before importing.
- Retrieve accessible public pages or use official APIs, owner-provided account exports and suitable archival snapshots where available. Verify current primary documentation, access requirements and supported record formats when implementing each adapter; this plan assumes no current endpoint or authentication behavior. Recovery is a local operation, and builds and runtime require no social-platform or archive connectivity.
- Keep raw exports, nonselected records, sessions and credentials outside Git and deployment. Normalize only selected public authored records and their reviewed provenance locally. Exclude private messages and nonselected private records; an export's availability does not authorize their publication. No cookies or passwords are requested by this planning item.
- Preserve original text, language, links, timestamps and their precision, media/captions, publisher URLs and stable platform identities. Keep source edit/version evidence and unresolved conflicts; conversion removes platform navigation, scripts and tracking rather than silently rewriting the author's content. Store selected media locally, preserve attribution and rights, and document missing assets.
- Facebook recovery preserves individual post content and evidenced chronology. Thread reconstruction applies to Twitter and Bluesky: use explicit root, parent and reply identities as primary evidence, retaining each post's identity, author and time. Quoted posts and other authors' replies remain distinct references; permission to republish their bodies or media is not inferred.
- Preserve branching replies as branches or a clearly explained ordered presentation with reply links. Missing roots, deleted posts, inaccessible parents and gaps remain explicit. Never invent bridging text, silently flatten uncertain relationships or assign another author's content to Dario.
- Related posts beyond explicit reply chains, including cross-platform copies, may form labeled editorial collections with recorded relationship evidence. Preserve their individual identities, times and original order within each source sequence; distinguish these collections from authentic threads. Topic or time similarity can suggest an editorial collection for review, but never proves an original reply/thread relationship.

## Acceptance

- The inventory records selected source/account identity, stable post IDs and URLs, authorship/public-status evidence, available versions, media and relationships, plus a disposition: already local, recovered and reviewed, pending evidence/rights, or unavailable. Reconcile totals for source records, reconstructed threads, editorial collections and local documents without treating each as the same count.
- Reviewed provenance records source and snapshot URLs/capture times, retrieval times, saved-byte hashes, original publication evidence/precision, edit evidence, conversion changes, attribution and missing content/media. Curated provenance stays out of deployment; raw exports and session material remain outside the repository.
- Each proposed local document contains reviewed Markdown and necessary local media, with original source links and individual post boundaries. Authentic threads retain evidenced root/parent links and gaps; editorial collections state their grouping rationale. Do not silently discard conflicting cross-platform versions or deduplicate separate identities merely because text matches.
- Apply T04 date conventions to individual posts and aggregates. An authentic thread uses its source root's original publication date when known; later replies retain their own timestamps. Capture, export and import dates never replace publication. An unknown root date or incomplete relationship remains explicit; use only supported partial-date conventions or retain the item in the unpublished inventory until it can be represented accurately. Editorial collection dates and their basis are reviewed separately from constituent publication dates.
- New recovered pages default to `feed: false` and `atproto: false` until provenance, dates and content are reviewed and syndication is explicitly selected. Recovery never enlarges T30's approved selection. Preserve existing URLs, RSS GUIDs, exclusions and Standard.site identities when an already-local item is reconciled.
- Imports use stable source identities and hashes to prevent duplicate posts/assets and make unchanged reimports produce no changes. Proposed version updates, relationship changes and cross-platform matches appear in a dry-run manifest/diff for review; a failed batch leaves no partial import.

Reconcile the inventory and local output counts.
- If extraction tooling is needed, later substantive fixtures cover each supported platform input, stable identities, escaping/sanitization, edits/conflicting versions, missing media, partial dates, explicit reply chains, branches, deleted/missing roots, quotes, cross-platform copies, idempotence and failure without partial writes. This planning item introduces no adapter, scraper or recovery tests.
- After each reviewed import batch, run T02's guarded build and T31's consistency gate. Compare canonical paths, archive entries, publication precision and existing RSS GUIDs/state; confirm recovered exclusions in RSS and Standard.site selection and absence of private provenance, exports and session material from deploy output.
- With saved evidence and cached build dependencies, build without Facebook, Twitter, Bluesky or archive connectivity. Verify local media availability and retain the previous deployment if validation fails.

## Commits and operational actions

Each Git commit includes the implementation, its necessary tests and its documentation. Zas commits become focused PRs against current upstream master. External project source changes require their own authorized repository work; preparation artifacts belong in the site repository.

- **site** · `docs(social): inventory selected historical posts and relationships`: Reviewed source scope, curated provenance/normalization conventions, source dispositions and thread/collection evidence, reusing T33's shared recovery approach without checking in raw exports.
- **site**, only if needed · `feat(tools): extract reviewed <platform> posts`: Small platform-specific adapter/extraction commits with primary-documentation verification, dry-run manifests, sanitization, stable identities, substantive fixtures and operator instructions. Keep extraction separate from reviewed content batches; no build-time fetching.
- **site** · `feat(posts): recover <reviewed thread, item or source batch>`: Atomic reviewed Markdown, local media, curated provenance, relationship presentation, metadata and archive changes together. Preserve syndication exclusions until explicit selection; later Standard.site proposals follow T30's reviewed-selection procedure.

## Rollback

Revert an affected reviewed import and its archive/asset additions together, retaining other items, established URLs, GUIDs and publishing state. Revert optional adapters independently and preserve source evidence outside Git for a corrected reimport. Restore the previous successful build if necessary; any later remote record deletion follows its separately approved explicit-target procedure and cannot revoke third-party copies.
