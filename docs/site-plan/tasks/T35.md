# T35 Compile Dario's press and media archive

Compile a sourced chronological hemeroteca about Dario Castañé: coverage, interviews, profiles and verified substantive mentions. Expand the existing Catalan archive after inventory and source review; this queued plan starts no press research, retrieval or publication.

**Repository:** site. **Phase:** Content archive. **Depends on:** T04, T02, T16, T31 (already available). T33/T34 recovery is not a prerequisite.

**Status:** Queued. **Subagent:** /root/t35_hemeroteca (task planning).

## Repository findings and scope

- [Existing Hemeroteca](../../../references/index.md) already establishes `/references/` and contains one Pirates de Catalunya link under a 2020 heading. Treat it as a repository lead, not a live-verified source or verified publication date. Preserve this public URL; consider `/hemeroteca/` only as a later alias to the same archive, avoiding duplicate pages.
- Begin the inventory with existing references, [talks](../../../talks/index.md), [articles](../../../posts/index.md) and [publication provenance](../../publication-provenance.md). They supply source leads and reconciliation evidence, not a claim that further press appearances have been discovered. Owner-supplied sources can refine the scope later.
- Verify each item's connection to Dario using source content and existing identity evidence. Record evidenced name variants and supporting context; a matching name or search snippet alone cannot establish identity. Keep disputed or unsupported matches outside the public archive.
- Include third-party coverage, interviews, profiles and substantive mentions with a recorded inclusion rationale. Dario's authored articles belong to T33 and his social posts to T34. Cross-reference existing local articles/talks when relevant instead of listing the same appearance twice; a distinct report about a talk may remain a separate item.
- Preserve original title, outlet, author when available, language, source type, original URL and publication evidence/precision. Prefer the original source; record suitable archive.org links and capture times when needed, plus retrieval time, saved evidence/provenance, discrepancies and unavailable pieces. Capture, event and retrieval dates never replace publication dates.
- Group evidenced syndicated copies as one underlying piece while retaining their outlet/source links. Distinguish these from separate interviews, reports or appearances; similar titles or text alone do not justify silently merging records.

## Acceptance

- Every inventory item has identity and inclusion evidence, provenance and a disposition: reviewed for inclusion, already represented locally, pending evidence, excluded or unavailable. Reconcile counts by underlying piece and by source appearance, including syndicated copies and cross-references.
- The public Catalan `/references/` page uses the shared archive list style, newest-first chronology and supported partial-date presentation. Preserve original titles and languages, label outlets and source types, and add only short source-grounded descriptions. Items without a verified calendar date remain in the unpublished inventory until they can be represented accurately; never fabricate a day, clock or timezone.
- Original and archived links have accessible contextual labels identifying the piece and destination. An unavailable original remains explicitly labeled when archived evidence supports inclusion; unavailable items without adequate evidence stay in the inventory rather than becoming unsupported public claims.
- Link to third-party material. Inclusion does not authorize reproducing full text, photographs or other media, or applying the site's license to them. Any later excerpt or media proposal needs its own rights review and attribution; keep private provenance and saved evidence outside deployment.
- Preserve existing article/talk URLs, archive entries, RSS GUIDs and Standard.site identities. An archive citation does not become an authored post, feed entry or Standard.site publication; T33/T34 imports and syndication choices remain separate.

## Verification

- Review each proposed citation against saved original/snapshot evidence for identity, title, outlet, author, language, date precision, description and source links. Check syndicated grouping, distinct appearances and local cross-references independently; record unresolved conflicts and retrieval failures.
- After each reviewed archive change, run T02's guarded build and T31's consistency gate. Inspect the rendered `/references/` page for chronology, shared styling, language labels, contextual link names and source-derived metadata. Check any later alias resolves to the established archive without a competing canonical page.
- Confirm private evidence stays absent from deployment, existing vanity routes and article/talk URLs survive, and RSS/Standard.site selections remain unchanged. With saved evidence and cached build dependencies, building the archive requires no publisher or archive.org connectivity. No scraper or recovery tests are introduced by this planning item.
