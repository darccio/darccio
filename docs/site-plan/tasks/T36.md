# T36 Review and polish the website item by item

Review every public page and shared UI, prioritizing owner-reported artifacts such as footnotes. Record each finding and correct it as a small reviewable unit. This item queues the audit and fixes; the planning addition changes no templates, styles or authored public content.

**Repository:** site; Zas only for demonstrated generic renderer defects. **Phase:** UI/UX polish. **Depends on:** T02, T09, T16, T17, T24, T31 and completed shared design work.

**Status:** Queued. **Subagent:** /root/t36_ux_review (task planning).

## Inventory and review scope

- Reconcile source, route manifests and guarded built output into a complete public route inventory grouped by page type and locale: home/navigation, slash and identity pages, article archives and individual posts, talks, references, CV, project landings and fetched docs. Include aliases, historical URLs, rendered twins and machine/resource routes with their applicable checks; identify raw vanity fragments explicitly.
- Account for Catalan root content, English translations and Spanish content, including pages without translations. Review the shared shell and every page's actual content; sampling shared components does not replace page-by-page disposition.
- Inspect typography, wrapping, contrast, spacing and hierarchy; reading order and flow; navigation, current-page state, language switching and destination locale; contextual link/resource labels; image/media sizing and alternatives; lists, tables, code, archives, CV sections and project docs navigation.
- Inspect rendered footnotes against authored sources: numbering and marker placement, unique IDs, destination/backlink integrity, accessible labels, spacing and reading flow. Check orphan markers, duplicate generated labels or IDs, return icons and untranslated generated text across assembled content; identify leaked or stray generation markup elsewhere. Preserve authored references, citations and existing URLs/fragments; propose any necessary compatibility-preserving correction explicitly.
- Reuse the chosen design tokens and completed design work, including Newsreader headings, Plex Sans body and Mono UI. Polish demonstrated inconsistencies without introducing a speculative redesign, new client JavaScript or new preferences that block this queued task.

## Evidence and issue register

- Give every reviewed route an explicit outcome, including clean/no-issue pages. Maintain reconciled totals by page type and locale so no page disappears behind shared-component findings.
- Give each finding a stable ID (for example, UX-001), affected routes/locales, interaction state, viewport/theme/text setting, reproduction steps, screenshot or equivalent evidence and exact source-to-output mapping. Distinguish owner-reported concerns, verified defects and observations needing evidence.
- Record severity and reader impact, proposed smallest fix, repository/component owner, acceptance criteria, related finding IDs and disposition/status. Link each fix to its commit/PR and before/after evidence; retain explicit rationale for a deferred, accepted or inapplicable finding.
- Separate shared template/style/component defects from page-specific content defects. Verify every affected route after a shared fix and avoid silently closing distinct issues under a broad cosmetic change.

## Implementation boundaries

- Preserve Catalan/English/Spanish content and language metadata, public routes/fragments, canonical URLs, microformats, JSON-LD, original date precision, RSS GUIDs, Standard.site identities and CV JSON. Preserve all seven exact vanity contracts and raw fragment bytes.
- Presentation or footnote corrections may change rendered feed bodies or justified update dates; record those effects and verify cross-format agreement. Keep datePublished and feed GUIDs unchanged; never manufacture publication or update dates merely to satisfy a checker.
- Keep site templates, styling, authored content and assembly fixes here. A demonstrated generic Zas renderer defect needs its own focused upstream PR, necessary regression coverage and a tested reviewed pin before site adoption; do no generator work without evidence requiring it.
- Keep existing publish:false planning and operational material excluded from deployment. Use new unpublished review evidence and the existing PROGRESS receipt workflow; do not turn internal findings or screenshots into public pages accidentally.
- External media availability is a separate evidenced condition. Record external-asset failures and their impact; the missing Cobi photo needs an authentic owner-supplied or otherwise authorized source, not an invented replacement or an unsupported claim of resolution.

## Acceptance and verification

- The complete inventory has a reviewed disposition for every route, and every finding has a final acceptance/status record. No discovered material issue remains unresolved without explicit recorded rationale, impact and next action; distinguish verified fixes from external or owner dependencies.
- Trace each changed source through guarded built output, a concrete preview and deployed production. Record revision, route, time and scope for each environment; do not describe a local result as production verification or infer a deployed fix from source alone.
- After each fix, perform focused browser checks for the affected content, routes, locale/state and shared-component consumers, with comparable before/after evidence. Verify footnote links and backlinks by following them, source fidelity and fragment compatibility, plus absence of stray markup where relevant.
- Run the full guarded build and appropriate tests, exact vanity, mf2 and T31 consistency checks. Check machine/resource content, MIME and path-specific behavior because unknown live resource URLs may return the homepage with 200; HTTP success alone is insufficient.
- Finish with site-wide axe checks in light and dark at 1440px and 390px, plus 320px with normal and doubled text. Manually review reflow, reading order, contrast, keyboard navigation, visible focus, link purpose and locale switching/current state. Axe results alone do not establish accessibility.
- Raw vanity fragments intentionally lack the shared visual shell: verify their exact contracts without styling them into ordinary pages. Verify project docs and CV separately, including navigation, overflow and preserved source/JSON boundaries. Record external-asset, unavailable-photo and browser/tool limits honestly.

## Commits and operational actions

Follow the plan's staging, darna, hook and PROGRESS rules. Each later change carries its necessary meaningful checks and evidence; avoid tests that merely mirror a cosmetic implementation.

- **site** · `docs(ux): record complete page review and findings`: Route inventory, clean outcomes, reproducible findings, severity, source mapping and proposed acceptance, kept unpublished.
- **site** · `fix(ui): correct a verified shared presentation issue`: One coherent shared-template/style fix with affected-route checks and before/after evidence; keep separate from page-specific content corrections.
- **site** · `fix(content): correct a verified page presentation issue`: Small coherent page/content units, preserving references, URLs, identities and publication facts; include any rendered-feed/update-date effects in review evidence.
- **zas, if evidenced** · Separate atomic renderer fix PRs with regression tests and documentation, followed by a separately reviewed site pin/adoption unit and full guards.

## Rollback

Revert the affected atomic fix and its paired assertions together, retaining the review register and evidence for correction. Roll back a generator adoption independently to the previous tested pin. Restore the previous successful deploy if guards fail, preserving unrelated fixes, all established URLs/fragments, authored references and publishing identities.
