## Pairing and exclusions

- The cloud-computing deck and repository presentation match; list them together.
- The esLibre short Freedom to Share deck and Commons recording belong together. The longer repository version names WORLDPARTY2k21; its separate YouTube excerpt remains a distinct entry. Its original linked recording is unavailable, so the uploader’s exact day is not promoted to a verified event date.
- Autodefensa Digital’s Speaker Deck presentation matches the standalone submodule; the later security conference has a separate recording and source variant.
- The 12 owner-supplied YouTube links are covered exactly once each. Open Mic uses the owner-supplied title for the second talk; no chapter timestamp is exposed, so its public link identifies the second talk in text. Its organiser gives 2020-06-03 at 19:00 online. This is a separate Mergo appearance from GoDevNet.
- The organiser’s Microserveis recording identifies Dario’s Contenidors segment and pairs with the 2018-09-12 deck; its 2018-09-18 upload is separate. Techno Politics and Municipi recordings pair with their respective same-title decks. Municipio’s live release metadata says 2016-02-20, but the catalogue omits an exact event day without programme confirmation and retains the verified Catalan deck language despite conflicting automatic recording metadata.
- Gobierno abierto is a distinct Spanish talk in El Arenal, Ávila; no exact event date is established.
- The 2019 Tecnologies disruptives deck and 2020 Mobile Week presentation remain separate. Only the 2019 event month is verified in the deck; its publication day is not used as the event day.
- Orchestrion’s official GoLab video confirms 2024-11-12. The linked source is now adapted for APM Ecosystems Virtual IC Summit 25Q1; do not assign that adapted deck the GoLab date as an independent event.
- YouTube declares Spanish for Generics and English for Mergo/Orchestrion. RCR explicitly labels CAT. Other video titles/descriptions suggest language but do not prove spoken language; the public catalogue omits recording-only language labels in those cases.
- Exclude engineering/index.md (research notes), theme/build files, incidental assets and generated output; these do not establish additional talks.
